# ARC BRAND LOCKUP SPEC V1.0

STATUS: ACTIVE-DEV
DATE: 2026-09-18

ARC is the master brand. CORE, N°, and FINAL are equal sub-brand categories.

Rules:
- Keep the ARC wordmark identical in size and geometry across CORE / N° / FINAL.
- Keep the burgundy arc symbol identical across all variants.
- Keep divider length, divider position, label baseline, spacing, and overall lockup proportions identical.
- ARC wordmark: Deep Navy #1F2A44.
- Arc symbol: Burgundy #7A2E35.
- CORE label/divider: print-safe warm gray #8A877F.
- N° label/divider: Deep Navy #1F2A44.
- FINAL label/divider: Burgundy #7A2E35.
- CORE, N°, FINAL are compact secondary editorial labels; never equal in visual weight to ARC.
- N° may receive up to 10% optical glyph enlargement while preserving the same label-zone bounding box and baseline.
- The degree sign remains a superscript.
- Cover volume number such as N° 01 is separate metadata, not part of the logo geometry.
- Internal running heads may use text-only ARC CORE / ARC N°01 / ARC FINAL.
- Do not resize ARC per product, move the arc symbol, change arc color, vary divider length, add gradients/shadows/3D effects, or add another icon.
- Minimum-size print test is required before final release.

END ARC BRAND LOCKUP SPEC V1.0
